About Us

We study AI's societal implications, build a community of engaged students and researchers, and equip people to take meaningful action.

Societal AI is a community that explores the intersection of artificial intelligence with society, studies what we can do to maximize benefits while minimizing harms, and mobilizes people to take action for AI safety.

We come from a variety of backgrounds, from computer science to philosophy, but all care about one thing: AI having a positive impact on society.
