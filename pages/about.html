About Us

We study AI's societal implications, build a community of engaged students and researchers, and equip people to take meaningful action.

Our Purpose

Societal AI at UVA is a professional student organization dedicated to ensuring that AI is developed and deployed in ways that benefit society. Through educational workshops, roundtable discussions, guest speaker series, and hands-on events, we pursue two core goals.

Our Values

We aim to build a multidisciplinary community where UVA students and faculty can engage with AI primarily through humanistic and social lenses. The future of AI belongs to everyone, not just tech people — no matter your background, you are welcome here.

Our Mission

We serve as a pipeline for UVA students — particularly those from non-technical backgrounds — to gain relevant knowledge, build individual portfolios, and pursue opportunities in the growing interdisciplinary fields surrounding AI.
