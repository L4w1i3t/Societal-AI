Other Resources

AI, data science, and technology organizations across UVA

Beyond Societal AI, there's a whole ecosystem of student organizations, research labs, and academic programs at UVA working on AI, data, and technology. Explore them below.

This is a non-exhaustive repository of opportunities on-grounds in AI — there may be groups we didn't list.

Student Organizations & Clubs

Societal AI

A UVA student community that studies AI's societal implications and mobilizes students to take action for AI safety.

Virginia AI Security Initiative

A UVA community dedicated to mitigating the risks of advanced AI through education, research opportunities, and career pathways in AI safety and governance.

Business and AI Institute

Builds a community of future leaders who understand both the technical and business implications of AI through workshops, internships, and industry partnerships.

ML@UVA

UVA's machine learning and AI organization, offering workshops, original research projects, and industry partnerships for 200+ student members.

Data Science and Analytics Club

A student-led club that brings undergraduates and graduates together to explore data science through networking, workshops, and tutoring.

Computer and Network Security Club

A group of UVA students interested in software, network, hardware, and application security who compete in competitions and host speakers.

Law, Innovation, Security & Technology Society

A UVA Law student organization exploring the legal, policy, and business challenges raised by emerging technologies like AI, privacy, and cybersecurity.

Student Technology Council

A student-elected body that advocates for technology governance and innovation on behalf of the UVA student body.

Research Centers, Labs & Institutes

Sloane Lab

An interdisciplinary research group studying the social, cultural, and political implications of artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies.

EconTAI

Delivers economic research and insights to help navigate the transition to transformative AI and guide it toward shared prosperity.

Link Lab

A UVA Engineering research center of 350+ faculty and students advancing cyber-physical systems, from robotics to smart cities.

AI + Human Flourishing Initiative

Studies the two-way relationship between AI and human wellbeing to inform more human-centered alignment strategies.

Digital Technology for Democracy Lab

A university-wide collaboration examining how digital technologies challenge, and can strengthen, democratic institutions.

AI Research @ UVA

Connects AI research happening across all twelve UVA schools and works to cultivate the next generation of AI researchers.

LaCross Institute for Ethical AI in Business

A UVA Darden institute equipping business leaders with the ethics and analytics skills needed for responsible AI development.

Academic Departments & Programs

Department of Computer Science

UVA's academic department offering B.S., B.A., and minor programs in computer science, including AI-focused coursework and research.

School of Data Science

UVA's school offering data science degrees at every level, focused on teaching and practicing responsible data science and AI for the common good.
