Fall AI Fair

Participating Organizations

Welcome! Here's who you'll find tabling at the Fall AI Fair, along with a one-sentence overview and a link to each organization's website.

Societal AI

A UVA student community that studies AI's societal implications and mobilizes students to take action for AI safety.

Virginia AI Security Initiative

A UVA community dedicated to mitigating the risks of advanced AI through education, research opportunities, and career pathways in AI safety and governance.

Business and AI Institute

Builds a community of future leaders who understand both the technical and business implications of AI through workshops, internships, and industry partnerships.

ML@UVA

UVA's machine learning and AI organization, offering workshops, original research projects, and industry partnerships for 200+ student members.

Data Science and Analytics Club

A student-led club that brings undergraduates and graduates together to explore data science through networking, workshops, and tutoring.

School of Data Science

UVA's school offering data science degrees at every level, focused on teaching and practicing responsible data science and AI for the common good.

Sloane Lab

An interdisciplinary research group studying the social, cultural, and political implications of artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies.

Student Technology Council

A student-elected body that advocates for technology governance and innovation on behalf of the UVA student body.
